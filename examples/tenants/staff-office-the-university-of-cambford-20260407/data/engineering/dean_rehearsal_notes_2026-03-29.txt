# Dean's Rehearsal Notes - 2026-03-29

## Author: Hiroshi Matsuda

### Context
This document contains detailed notes and feedback from the dean's additional rehearsal for his opening lecture, intended to be delivered during the orientation day for STEM majors. The rehearsal was conducted in response to the dean's concerns about the flow and delivery of his presentation.

---

### Observations

#### General Presentation Flow
- **Introduction**:
  - The opening segment felt slightly rushed. The dean seemed eager to move into the technical content but missed an opportunity to establish rapport with the audience.
  - Recommendation: Incorporate a brief anecdote or engaging story to create a stronger emotional connection.

- **Main Content**:
  - The dean’s transitions between topics were clear but could benefit from smoother phrasing to maintain audience engagement.
  - The section discussing the university’s initiatives on renewable energy was well-received but slightly data-heavy. Consider simplifying key statistics to avoid overwhelming the audience.

- **Conclusion**:
  - The closing remarks lacked a memorable takeaway message. The dean primarily reiterated earlier points without tying them together effectively.
  - Recommendation: Use a compelling call-to-action or a visionary statement to leave a lasting impression.

#### Delivery Style
- **Voice Modulation**:
  - While confident, the dean’s tone was consistently formal. Introducing subtle variations in tone and volume could make the presentation more dynamic.

- **Body Language**:
  - Positive: The dean maintained eye contact and used open gestures, which created a welcoming atmosphere.
  - Improvement Area: The pacing of movement across the stage was uneven, occasionally distracting.
  - Recommendation: Practice deliberate, slower movements to command attention and emphasize key points.

#### Audiovisual Setup
- **Slide Deck**:
  - Strengths:
    - Slides were visually appealing and aligned with the university’s branding.
    - The use of graphics and charts effectively supported complex topics.
  - Weaknesses:
    - Font size on some slides was too small for audience members seated at the back.
    - Recommendation: Increase font size and simplify slide content for improved readability.

- **Microphone and Sound Testing**:
  - The wireless microphone occasionally picked up static. The logistics team was informed and resolved the issue by replacing the unit.

- **Lighting**:
  - Lighting was adequate but could be improved to ensure the dean remains well-lit throughout the lecture.

---

### Feedback from Observers
- **Staff Members**:
  - “The dean’s passion for engineering is clear, but the presentation could benefit from a more personal touch.”
  - “The visuals are impressive, but simplifying some graphs would make the data more digestible.”

- **Student Ambassadors**:
  - “Adding a humorous or relatable anecdote would make the lecture feel less formal and more engaging.”
  - “The call-to-action at the end could be stronger—it felt a bit generic.”

---

### Action Items

#### Adjustments to Content
- Refine the introduction to include a personal anecdote.
- Simplify key data points in the renewable energy section.
- Develop a stronger, more impactful closing statement.

#### Delivery Practice
- Conduct additional rehearsals focusing on voice modulation and stage pacing.
- Experiment with varying tone and volume to maintain audience engagement.

#### Audiovisual Enhancements
- Update slide deck to increase font size and simplify complex visuals.
- Confirm the functionality of all audiovisual equipment prior to the orientation day.
- Coordinate with the logistics team to optimize lighting setup.
