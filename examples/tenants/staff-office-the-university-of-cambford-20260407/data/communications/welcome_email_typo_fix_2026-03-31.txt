## Overview
This document details the identification and correction of a typo in the subject line of the welcome email for incoming students, scheduled for mass distribution on 2026-03-31. The typo was identified during the final content review process by the communications team and resolved immediately to ensure proper messaging and maintain professional standards.

## Details of the Issue

- **Subject Line with Typo:** "Welcom to Campus! Here's What You Need to Know."
  - The word "Welcom" was missing the final "e."
- **Intended Subject Line:** "Welcome to Campus! Here's What You Need to Know."
- **Impact:**
  - The typo, while minor, could have negatively affected the university's professional image.
  - It was imperative to fix the issue before the email was sent to over 5,000 recipients, including new students, parents, and staff.

## Steps Taken to Resolve the Issue

1. **Identification:**
   - The typo was flagged during the routine proofreading step by a communications team member at 11:45 AM.

2. **Immediate Action:**
   - The email draft was immediately pulled from the scheduled distribution queue in the university’s email marketing platform (MailSender Pro).

3. **Correction Process:**
   - The subject line was updated in the email template to include the correct spelling of "Welcome."
   - A second proofreading round was conducted to ensure no additional issues were present in the email body or subject line.

4. **Verification:**
   - A test email was sent to the communications team at 12:15 PM to confirm the correction.
   - Team members verified that the subject line appeared correctly in various email clients (Gmail, Outlook, mobile, etc.).

5. **Rescheduling:**
   - The corrected email was re-added to the distribution queue at 12:45 PM.
   - The scheduled send time remained unchanged at 3:00 PM.

## Additional Notes

- **Coordination:**
  - The communications team worked closely with the IT department to ensure no additional technical issues arose during the email rescheduling process.
- **Lessons Learned:**
  - Moving forward, the communications team will implement an additional proofreading step focused on subject lines before emails are queued for delivery.
  - A checklist for email quality assurance will be created to prevent similar issues.

## Supporting Data

- **Number of Recipients Affected:** 5,243 (new students, parents, and staff).
- **Time Spent Resolving the Issue:** Approximately 1 hour (11:45 AM - 12:45 PM).
- **Email Marketing Platform Used:** MailSender Pro.
- **Testing Details:**
  - Email clients tested: Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo Mail, and default mobile email apps (iOS and Android).
  - Results: All tests confirmed the corrected subject line displayed as intended.

## Follow-Up Actions

- **Post-Distribution Review:**
  - The communications team will monitor the email’s performance metrics (open rate, click-through rate, etc.) to ensure no further issues impact its effectiveness.

- **Process Update:**
  - An updated email proofreading and quality assurance process will be drafted and shared with the team by 2026-04-07.

- **Acknowledgment:**
  - Special thanks to the team member who identified the typo and the IT department for their support in ensuring timely resolution.
