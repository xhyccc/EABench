## 1. Overview of Pre-IPO Goals

### Objectives:
- Ensure competitive equity offerings to attract top-tier talent post-IPO.
- Align stock option strategies with long-term growth projections.
- Mitigate risks associated with dilution and shareholder dissatisfaction.

### Key Metrics:
- Projected IPO valuation: **$1.2B - $1.5B**.
- Employee retention rate (current): **87%**.
- Subscription retention metrics (recent inquiry): **94%**.

---

## 2. Current Equity Structure

### Summary:
- **Total shares outstanding:** 20,000,000.
- **Employee Stock Option Pool (ESOP):** 15% allocated (3,000,000 shares).
- **Founders and Early Investors:** 55%.
- **Preferred Shares (Series A, B, C):** 30%.

### Challenges:
- Need to expand ESOP allocation to 18-20% without significant dilution.
- Address concerns from potential investors about cap table complexity.

---

## 3. Stock Option Strategy Recommendations

### Immediate Actions:
- Increase ESOP pool allocation to 18% to support hiring plans.
- Implement a **tiered vesting schedule** to incentivize long-term retention:
  - Year 1: 25% vesting (cliff).
  - Years 2-4: Monthly vesting (remaining 75%).

### Long-Term Strategy:
- Introduce **performance-based stock options** for key leadership roles.
- Consider **restricted stock units (RSUs)** post-IPO to reduce tax burden and simplify administration.

### Benchmarking:
- Comparable companies in the SaaS sector offer ESOP pools of **15-20%** pre-IPO.
- Performance-based stock options are increasingly common among high-growth startups.

---

## 4. Tax Implications and Compliance

### Key Considerations:
- Ensure compliance with **Internal Revenue Code Section 409A** for stock option valuations.
- Evaluate **tax implications** for international employees, especially in regions with high tax rates.

### Recommendations:
- Conduct a **409A valuation** prior to expanding the ESOP pool.
- Partner with a global tax consultancy to address cross-border compliance challenges.
- Communicate clearly with employees about the tax implications of exercising options.
