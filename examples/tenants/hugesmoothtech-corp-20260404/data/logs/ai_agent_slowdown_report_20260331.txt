- **Increase Monitoring:**
  - Deploy enhanced monitoring tools to capture detailed performance metrics and identify bottlenecks in real-time.
- **Communicate with Teams:**
  - Issue a transparent internal memo explaining the issue and outlining steps being taken to resolve it.

### 2. Short-Term Actions (Next 7 Days)
- **Conduct Root Cause Analysis:**
  - Assemble a task force to investigate the interplay between recent updates and system performance.
- **Implement Load Testing:**
  - Stress-test the AI coding agents under simulated peak-hour conditions to identify scalability issues.

### 3. Long-Term Actions

- **Employee Support:**
  - Address morale concerns by improving transparency around layoffs and prioritizing employee-requested features.
