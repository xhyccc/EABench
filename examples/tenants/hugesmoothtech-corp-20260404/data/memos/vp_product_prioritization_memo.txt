# Memo: Product Feature Reprioritization

**From:** Mariana Garcia, VP of Product  
**Date:** April 2, 2026  
**Subject:** Urgent Reprioritization of Features to Address Client Complaints  

---

## Context

Over the past week, HugeSmoothTech has faced significant challenges stemming from unresolved client concerns and deteriorating relationships with major accounts. These issues have culminated in threats of litigation, strained contractual negotiations, and a growing risk to our reputation in the enterprise SaaS market. Amid the ongoing internal shift toward an AI-first strategy, we cannot afford to neglect the fundamental usability and reliability of our product offerings.

### Key Issues
1. **Client Complaints**: Persistent product bugs in our SaaS platform have caused missed deadlines and operational disruptions for key clients. One client has escalated their dissatisfaction to the point of threatening legal action.
2. **Critical Bugs**: The recent updates to the AI-driven coding agent introduced critical bugs, leading to production build failures and emergency patches.
3. **Morale and Capacity**: Employee morale is at an all-time low due to layoffs, leaked plans for workforce reductions, and dissatisfaction with leadership decisions. Teams are overworked and struggling to meet deadlines under current conditions.
4. **Reputation Risk**: Negative press and online debates surrounding our AI-first strategy are amplifying public skepticism of HugeSmoothTech’s commitment to quality and customer satisfaction.

---

## Required Actions

To address these urgent issues, I am directing all product teams to immediately reprioritize development efforts. The following steps must be implemented without delay:

### 1. **Focus on Client-Critical Bugs**
- **Objective:** Resolve high-impact bugs affecting client operations.
- **Action Items:**
  - Assign priority status to all unresolved bugs flagged by client support teams.
  - Collaborate directly with engineering to expedite testing and deployment of fixes.
  - Establish a daily bug triage meeting to ensure progress is tracked and communicated.
- **Deadline:** April 6, 2026.

### 2. **Usability Enhancements**
- **Objective:** Address key usability issues identified in client feedback.
- **Action Items:**
  - Review the most frequent complaints logged in support tickets over the last quarter.
  - Implement quick-win improvements where feasible (e.g., UI tweaks, performance optimizations).
  - Schedule a client feedback session to validate changes and gather additional input.
- **Deadline:** April 15, 2026.

### 3. **Pause Low-Priority AI Features**
- **Objective:** Free up resources to focus on client needs.
- **Action Items:**
  - Temporarily halt development of non-critical AI-driven features.
  - Redirect engineering capacity toward bug fixes and usability enhancements.
  - Reassess the roadmap for AI features after immediate client concerns are resolved.
- **Deadline:** Ongoing; immediate implementation.

### 4. **Client Communication Plan**
- **Objective:** Restore trust with major accounts.
- **Action Items:**
  - Draft and distribute an apology letter acknowledging the disruptions and outlining our plans to address their concerns.
  - Schedule one-on-one calls with key clients to ensure transparency and accountability.
  - Provide weekly updates to clients on the status of bug fixes and product improvements.
- **Deadline:** Initial outreach by April 5, 2026, with ongoing updates.

---

## Metrics for Success

To ensure accountability and measure progress, teams will track the following metrics:

- **Bug Fix Rate**: Number of critical bugs resolved per week.
- **Client Satisfaction**: Improvement in client satisfaction scores based on post-resolution surveys.
- **Usability Metrics**: Reduction in support ticket volume related to usability issues.
- **Retention Rate**: Stabilization or improvement in client contract renewals.

---

## Final Notes

I recognize that these changes will place additional demands on already-stressed teams. However, the urgency of the situation requires immediate action to protect our relationships with key clients and stabilize HugeSmoothTech’s reputation in the market. Management will provide any necessary support to facilitate these efforts. Please escalate any concerns or resource requirements to me directly.

Thank you for your swift cooperation.
