# Internal Update: Postponement of HR Q&A Session

### Author: Mona Sharif  
### Date: 2026-03-30  

---

### Context
The HR Department regrets to inform employees that the Q&A session scheduled for this afternoon has been postponed. This session was intended to provide clarity and address concerns following recent organizational changes and announcements. However, unforeseen circumstances necessitated this delay.

---

### Details of the Postponement
- **Original Schedule**: 2026-03-30, 3:00 PM (Virtual Session via Zoom)
- **Reason for Postponement**: 
  - Internal discussions among leadership remain unresolved, requiring additional deliberation before addressing employee concerns.
  - Recent escalations regarding employee feedback and client complaints have redirected leadership's immediate focus.
- **New Schedule**: TBD (HR will announce a revised date by the end of this week).

---

### Key Background Information
This Q&A session was announced as part of HR's ongoing efforts to provide transparency and support during a challenging period of organizational changes. Below is a summary of notable events leading to the session:

#### Recent Challenges
1. **Layoffs and Workforce Reduction**:
   - An internal memo (2026-03-25) outlined the timeline for the next phase of SDE layoffs.
   - A leaked document (2026-03-28) suggested plans to expand layoffs to non-engineering teams, causing widespread concern.

2. **AI-Driven Workplace Initiatives**:
   - The HR department introduced an 'Adapting to Change in AI-Driven Workplaces' workshop, which received backlash for being tone-deaf amid job insecurity fears.
   - Employees have expressed dissatisfaction with the usability and reliability of AI-driven coding agents, as well as the company's prioritization of AI features over customer feedback.

3. **Client Relations and Product Issues**:
   - Multiple clients have reported dissatisfaction with the SaaS product, citing unresolved bugs and lack of customer-requested features. 
   - A key client threatened legal action (2026-03-30) if critical bugs are not resolved within 48 hours.

4. **Employee Sentiment**:
   - An anonymous survey (2026-03-29) found that over 70% of employees feel uncertain about their job security.
   - Tensions have risen following leaked documents, resignations, and perceived lack of acknowledgment from leadership.

---

### Next Steps
1. **Revised Q&A Session**:
   - HR will prioritize rescheduling the Q&A session and will ensure it addresses both pre-submitted and live questions from employees.
   - Employees are encouraged to continue submitting their concerns via the anonymous feedback form.

2. **Additional Support Initiatives**:
   - HR will explore offering smaller, department-specific Q&A forums to supplement the main session.
   - Efforts to improve communication between leadership and employees will be enhanced, including more frequent updates on key issues.

3. **Commitment to Transparency**:
   - Leadership is committed to addressing employee concerns with honesty and clarity.
   - Updates regarding layoffs, AI initiatives, and client relations will be communicated more consistently moving forward.
