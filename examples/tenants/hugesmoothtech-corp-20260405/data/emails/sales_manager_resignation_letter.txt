Dear [Recipient's Name],

I am writing to formally submit my resignation from my position as Sales Manager at HugeSmoothTech, effective two weeks from today, on April 17, 2026. This decision has not come lightly, but after considerable reflection, I have concluded that it is in my best interest to step away at this time.

Over the past five years, I have been proud to contribute to HugeSmoothTech's growth and success. Working with this talented and driven team has been an incredible experience, and I am grateful for the opportunities I have had to advance my career here. However, recent developments within the company have led me to reevaluate my place within the organization.

## Reasons for Resignation

### 1. **Stress and Burnout**
The ongoing organizational shifts and the increased demands placed on the sales team have created an environment of constant pressure. Over the past year, client concerns regarding the reliability and transparency of our AI-generated deliverables have escalated significantly. In turn, expectations for the sales team to mitigate these concerns have grown to an unsustainable level. Despite our best efforts, addressing these issues without adequate support has taken a toll on my mental health and well-being.

### 2. **Dissatisfaction with the AI-First Strategy**
While I recognize the potential of AI to revolutionize the tech industry, I have become increasingly disillusioned with the company's AI-first strategy. The heavy reliance on AI-driven tools, particularly in software development, has raised significant concerns among clients and employees alike. The recent glitch in the AI-powered coding agent and the subsequent delays for a major client project were emblematic of the risks associated with this approach.

The lack of transparency around AI's limitations and the growing dependence on AI-driven processes have eroded trust—not only among clients but also within the internal teams who must rely on these tools. The company's insistence on doubling down on AI, despite mounting evidence of its drawbacks, has left me questioning the long-term viability of this strategy.

### 3. **Morale and Culture**
Over the past few months, the atmosphere within HugeSmoothTech has shifted dramatically. The uncertainty surrounding potential layoffs, the ethical debates over workforce displacement, and the leaked internal memos have created an environment of fear and distrust. While I appreciate HR's efforts to mitigate these challenges through initiatives like 'AI Awareness Week' and the proposed 'AI Transition Support Program,' these measures have felt superficial and disconnected from the core issues at hand.

## Gratitude and Reflections

Despite my current concerns, I want to emphasize how much I value my time at HugeSmoothTech. I have learned invaluable skills, forged meaningful relationships, and had the privilege of working alongside some of the most talented professionals in the industry. I will always look back on my tenure here with a sense of gratitude and pride.

I am committed to ensuring a smooth transition during my remaining time with the company. I will do everything I can to support my team, assist in onboarding my replacement, and address any outstanding client concerns before my departure. Please let me know how I can best facilitate this process.

## Closing Statement

I hope that my decision to leave will serve as an opportunity for reflection on the challenges facing the company and the importance of addressing employee concerns. HugeSmoothTech has the potential to remain a leader in the tech industry, but I believe that balancing innovation with employee and client trust will be critical to its long-term success.

Thank you once again for the opportunities I have had to grow and thrive here. I wish HugeSmoothTech continued success in the years to come.

Sincerely,  
Jamal Okonkwo
