# AI Ethics Roundtable Announcement

### From the Desk of Bintu Kabongo, HR Department

Dear HugeSmoothTech Team,

As we navigate a period of transformative change driven by our AI-first strategy, we recognize the need to address the concerns, questions, and ethical considerations that many of you have raised. To foster a transparent and constructive dialogue, we are pleased to announce the upcoming **AI Ethics Roundtable**, an open forum designed to tackle key issues surrounding AI integration, workforce evolution, and its impact on our company culture.

---

### Event Details

- **Date:** Wednesday, April 9, 2026  
- **Time:** 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM (PDT)  
- **Location:** Main Conference Hall, Building A  
- **Virtual Access:** Livestream link to be shared via email  

---

### Purpose of the Roundtable

The AI Ethics Roundtable will serve as an opportunity to:

- **Address Employee Concerns:** Discuss the ethical implications of AI adoption, including workforce displacement, transparency, and decision-making processes.
- **Foster Collaboration:** Encourage open dialogue between employees, leadership, and AI experts.
- **Shape Policy:** Gather employee input to refine our AI ethics framework and long-term integration strategy.
- **Promote Understanding:** Clarify misconceptions about AI-driven initiatives and provide insights into their objectives and limitations.

---

### Agenda

1. **Opening Remarks** (15 minutes)  
   - Speaker: Bintu Kabongo, HR Director  
   - Topic: Overview of AI-first strategy and key challenges.  

2. **Employee Concerns Panel** (30 minutes)  
   - Panelists: Representatives from HR, AI Team, and Sales Team  
   - Topics for discussion:
     - Workforce displacement and job security.
     - Ethical considerations in AI decision-making.
     - Impacts on employee morale and company culture.

3. **Expert Insights** (30 minutes)  
   - Guest Speaker: Dr. Amara Singh, AI Ethics Researcher  
   - Topic: Best practices for ethical AI integration in corporate environments.

4. **Open Q&A Session** (40 minutes)  
   - Format: Employees can submit questions live or anonymously via pre-event survey.
   - Moderators: Bintu Kabongo and Dr. Amara Singh.

5. **Closing Remarks** (5 minutes)  
   - Speaker: Bintu Kabongo  
   - Topic: Next steps and how employees can stay involved.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. Why is this roundtable being organized now?
We acknowledge the growing concerns among employees regarding the ethical and operational implications of our AI-first strategy. This roundtable aims to address these concerns and create a platform for transparent dialogue.

#### 2. Will employee input influence future decisions?
Yes. The feedback collected during this event will directly inform updates to our AI ethics framework and workforce transition policies.

#### 3. Can I participate anonymously?
Absolutely. Submit questions anonymously via the pre-event survey or pose them during the live Q&A session using the virtual platform’s anonymous feature.
