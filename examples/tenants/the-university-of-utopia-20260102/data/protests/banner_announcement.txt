# Banner Announcement: Student Protest Event

## Author
**Amira Malek**

## Date
**2025-12-28**

## Context
The students of [University Name] have organized an event to unveil a massive banner calling for the resignation of the interim chancellor. This symbolic act is part of ongoing protests against tuition hikes, department mergers, financial mismanagement, and lack of transparency from university leadership. The banner unveiling will take place at the library building, a location chosen for its central visibility and significance to the campus community.

---

## Event Details
- **Date:** December 28, 2025
- **Time:** 3:00 PM
- **Location:** Library Building, South Wall
- **Organizers:** Student Advocacy Coalition (SAC)

---

## Objectives
The protest aims to:
- Demand immediate resignation of the interim chancellor.
- Highlight the administration’s failure to address the financial crisis and campus infrastructure issues.
- Amplify voices calling for transparent leadership and stakeholder inclusion in decision-making processes.
- Protest against tuition hikes, department mergers, and scholarship cuts.

---

## Schedule
1. **2:30 PM**: Gathering of participants at the library plaza.
   - Distribution of flyers and protest signs.
   - Volunteer coordination and final setup.
2. **3:00 PM**: Banner unveiling ceremony.
   - Speech by SAC President, **Elena Ruiz**.
   - Reading of the petition demanding the chancellor’s resignation.
   - Moment of silence honoring impacted students, faculty, and staff.
3. **3:30 PM**: March to the administration building.
   - Chants and slogans calling for leadership accountability.
   - Hand-delivery of the petition to administration representatives.
4. **4:00 PM**: Sit-in at administration building (optional for participants).
   - Peaceful occupation of the lobby until university responds to demands.

---

## Key Messages
### Protest Slogans
- “Transparency, Not Tuition Hikes!”
- “Leadership Accountability Now!”
- “Chancellor Resign!”
- “Save Our Scholarships!”
- “Fix the Budget, Not Departments!”

### Banner Design
- **Dimensions:** 30 ft x 10 ft
- **Materials:** Recycled cloth, eco-friendly paints
- **Message:** “Chancellor Resign Now!”
  - Bold red and black lettering for maximum visibility.
  - University emblem with a slash through it, symbolizing dissatisfaction.
  - QR code linking to the petition and a detailed explanation of the protest.

---

## Recent Developments
- **Petition Update:** Exceeded 10,000 signatures as of December 27.
- **Alumni Coalition:** Conditional $100,000 donation for scholarships contingent on transparency measures.
- **Faculty Support:** Draft of no-confidence vote circulating among staff.
- **IT Crisis:** Ongoing outages delaying grades and campus operations.
- **Media Coverage:** Local journalists investigating financial mismanagement and anonymous donations.
