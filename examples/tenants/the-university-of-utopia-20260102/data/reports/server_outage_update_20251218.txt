# Server Outage Update - December 18, 2025

**Author:** Javier Garcia  
**Date:** December 18, 2025  

## Context
The university is currently facing a significant server outage that has disrupted online grading systems during finals week. This outage has added to the challenges already posed by an ongoing financial crisis and administrative unrest, further straining the university community. The IT department is actively working to resolve the issue, but the outage has caused widespread frustration among students, faculty, and staff.

## Key Details
- **Date of Outage:** December 17, 2025
- **Systems Affected:** Online grading platforms, including student and instructor portals.
- **Impact:**
  - Instructors are unable to submit final grades.
  - Students are unable to view their grades, leading to confusion and anxiety during finals week.
  - Administrative staff are unable to access critical grading and reporting systems.
- **Estimated Resolution Time:** 48 hours (expected by December 19, 2025).

## IT Department Findings
- **Root Cause:**
  - The server outage has been attributed to the failure of outdated hardware that had been flagged earlier in the year as a critical risk.
  - Insufficient maintenance and lack of funding for IT infrastructure upgrades exacerbated the issue.
- **Current Status:**
  - IT teams have identified the affected systems and are working to replace faulty components.
  - External consultants have been brought in to assist with the recovery process.

## Immediate Actions Taken
1. **Communication:**
   - Urgent notifications were sent to all faculty, staff, and students via email and SMS on December 17, 2025.
   - Updates will continue to be provided every 6 hours until the issue is resolved.
2. **Temporary Solutions:**
   - Faculty have been advised to record grades offline for the time being.
   - A temporary hotline has been established for urgent grading-related inquiries.
3. **Technical Response:**
   - Replacement hardware is being procured and expedited to campus.
   - Backup servers are being assessed for deployment as a contingency.

## Broader Impact
- **On Students:**
  - Anxiety and confusion during finals week, as grades are critical for scholarship eligibility, graduation requirements, and academic standing.
  - Increased pressure on student support services, including mental health counseling.
- **On Faculty:**
  - Disruption in finalizing and submitting grades, leading to delays in course closure processes.
  - Challenges in communicating grade-related updates to students.
- **On Administration:**
  - Heightened scrutiny and criticism from the university community due to the timing of the outage amidst ongoing financial and leadership challenges.

## Next Steps
1. **IT Department:**
   - Continue to prioritize server restoration and system stability.
   - Conduct a post-mortem analysis of the outage to prevent future occurrences.
2. **University Leadership:**
   - Address concerns from students and faculty by holding an emergency town hall meeting.
   - Reassess IT infrastructure funding and prioritize necessary upgrades in the next budget cycle.
3. **Communication Plan:**
   - Issue a detailed public report outlining the causes of the outage and steps being taken to resolve it.
   - Provide regular updates to the campus community until systems are fully restored.

## Related Developments
- **Financial Crisis:**
  - The ongoing financial crisis has exacerbated the impact of the outage, as prior warnings from the IT department about infrastructure risks were not addressed due to budget constraints.
- **Student Protests:**
  - The outage has intensified frustrations among students already protesting tuition hikes and faculty layoffs.
  - A sit-in at the administration building has continued, with tensions rising between students and university staff.
- **Alumni Fundraising Campaign:**
  - The alumni coalition has raised $200,000 toward its $500,000 goal to support scholarships and prevent department cuts. This effort has provided some hope to the university community amidst the crisis.

## Conclusion
The server outage has highlighted critical weaknesses in the university's infrastructure and the broader challenges posed by the financial crisis. While immediate efforts are focused on resolving the technical issues, it is clear that long-term investments in IT systems are necessary to prevent similar disruptions in the future. The administration must also address the underlying concerns of the university community to rebuild trust and ensure stability moving forward.
