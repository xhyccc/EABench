# Statement from Mariam Abdul on the University Administration's Lack of Transparency

**Date:** December 15, 2025  
**Author:** Mariam Abdul, Philanthropist and Advocate for Educational Equity  

---

## Introduction

As someone deeply committed to the success of our local university and the broader mission of accessible, high-quality education, I feel compelled to publicly address the ongoing crisis engulfing the institution. The recent developments, particularly the administration's lack of transparency and accountability, have caused me great concern. These issues threaten not only the financial stability of the university but also its reputation, its community, and its ability to fulfill its mission to educate the next generation of leaders.

---

## Key Issues of Concern

### 1. Lack of Transparency in Financial Management

- **Delayed Financial Transparency Plan**: Despite repeated promises to publish a comprehensive financial transparency plan, the administration continues to delay its release. This has left students, faculty, alumni, and donors in the dark regarding the state of the university's finances.
- **Unexplained Accounting Discrepancies**: The finance department has uncovered multiple accounting discrepancies over the past two weeks, raising serious questions about the accuracy and integrity of the university's financial records.
- **Closed-Door Meetings**: Decisions with far-reaching implications are being made behind closed doors, as evidenced by the recent leaked recording of the chancellor's meeting with department heads.

### 2. The Ongoing Financial Crisis

The university's financial crisis did not emerge overnight, but the administration's response has been reactive rather than proactive. Key issues include:

- **Proposed Tuition Hikes**: The suggestion to increase tuition places an unfair burden on students and families already struggling to afford higher education.
- **Department Mergers and Layoffs**: These measures may provide short-term relief but threaten the long-term integrity and diversity of academic programs.
- **Infrastructure Failures**: Persistent IT outages and outdated systems are further eroding trust in the administration's ability to manage its resources effectively.

### 3. Erosion of Trust Among Stakeholders

- **Student and Faculty Protests**: The escalating protests, sit-ins, and petitions highlight the growing disconnect between the administration and the university community.
- **Media Scrutiny**: Negative coverage from local media outlets underscores the administration's failure to effectively communicate and address the concerns of its stakeholders.
- **Alumni and Donor Concerns**: As a donor, I share the concerns of many in the alumni community about the administration's ability to responsibly manage contributions and deliver on its promises.

---

## My Call to Action

I urge the university administration to take the following immediate steps to restore trust and confidence:

1. **Publish a Transparent Financial Report**
   - Provide a detailed breakdown of the university's budget, including revenue sources, expenditures, and any outstanding debts or liabilities.
   - Include an explanation of the discovered accounting discrepancies and steps being taken to prevent future errors.

2. **Engage Stakeholders in Decision-Making**
   - Create an inclusive task force of students, faculty, alumni, and donors to develop a collaborative financial recovery plan.
   - Host regular open forums to discuss major decisions and gather input from the community.

3. **Commit to Ethical Leadership**
   - Demonstrate accountability by addressing the concerns raised in the leaked recording and other recent controversies.
   - Reaffirm the university's commitment to its core values of integrity, transparency, and equity.

4. **Invest in Critical Infrastructure**
   - Allocate resources to urgently upgrade IT systems and address ongoing outages, which are disrupting academic and administrative functions.
   - Prioritize funding for essential academic and student support services over non-critical expenditures.

---

## Closing Thoughts

The current crisis represents a pivotal moment for the university. While the challenges are daunting, they also present an opportunity for transformative change. However, such change can only occur if the administration demonstrates the transparency, accountability, and ethical leadership that the university community so desperately needs.

As a philanthropist, I have supported this institution for many years because I believe in its mission and its potential to transform lives. But I cannot, in good conscience, continue to provide financial support to an administration that lacks the transparency and accountability necessary to steward those contributions responsibly. If meaningful steps are not taken to address these issues, I will have no choice but to reevaluate my future involvement.

To the students, faculty, alumni, and staff who are fighting for the future of this institution: I stand with you. Your passion, resilience, and determination inspire hope in these challenging times. Together, we can demand better and ensure that this university emerges stronger and more united than ever.
