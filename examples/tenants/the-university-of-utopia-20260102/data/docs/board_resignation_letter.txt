**Date:** December 8, 2025  

---

**To:** The Board of Trustees  
**Institution:** [University Name]  

**Subject:** Resignation from the Board of Trustees  

---

Dear Members of the Board,

It is with a heavy heart that I write to formally announce my resignation from my position as a member of the Board of Trustees, effective immediately. This decision has not been made lightly and stems from deeply personal reasons that require my full attention and focus at this time.

Serving on this board has been one of the greatest honors of my professional life. Over the years, I have witnessed the unwavering dedication of this institution to its mission of fostering academic excellence, inclusivity, and innovation. I am immensely proud of the role the board has played in shaping the future of [University Name], and I will always treasure the collaborative spirit and shared commitment of my fellow trustees.

However, recent developments within the university have further highlighted the complexities and challenges we face as stewards of this institution. While I firmly believe in the collective capability of this board and administration to navigate these turbulent times, I recognize that my current personal circumstances prevent me from fully contributing to the efforts required in addressing these pressing issues. In light of this, I believe it is in the best interest of the board and the university for me to step aside to allow for new perspectives and leadership to emerge.

I am confident that the board will continue to work tirelessly to overcome the financial and operational challenges currently facing the university. The proposals and discussions around potential tuition adjustments, department mergers, and long-term sustainability require thoughtful consideration and decisive action. I trust that my colleagues will approach these issues with the wisdom, empathy, and integrity that I have come to admire deeply during my tenure.

Please know that my departure does not diminish my ongoing support for [University Name] and its mission. I remain committed to the success of this institution and am willing to assist in any way possible during this transitional period. I will also continue to champion the university's values and vision as a proud member of its extended community.

In closing, I would like to express my profound gratitude to every member of this board, as well as to the faculty, staff, students, and alumni who make [University Name] such a remarkable institution. I am confident that, despite the current challenges, the university's brightest days lie ahead.

Thank you for the opportunity to serve, and I wish you all the best as you continue this important work.

Sincerely,  

**Samuel Johnson**
