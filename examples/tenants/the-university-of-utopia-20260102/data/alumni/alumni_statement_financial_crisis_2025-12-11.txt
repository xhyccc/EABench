# Alumni Association Statement on the University Financial Crisis

**Date:** December 11, 2025  
**Author:** Sarah Nguyen, Representative of the Alumni Association  

---

## Introduction

As representatives of the university’s alumni community, we feel it is our responsibility to address the ongoing financial crisis that has severely impacted the institution we hold dear. The past few months have been marked by mounting challenges, including financial mismanagement, lack of transparency, and growing unrest among students, faculty, and alumni. While we recognize the complexity of the situation, the alumni association is deeply disappointed in the administration's handling of this crisis thus far. 

In this statement, we highlight key concerns, outline the impact of current decisions on the university community, and propose actionable steps to restore trust and work toward sustainable solutions.

---

## Key Concerns

The alumni association has identified several areas of concern that urgently require the administration’s attention:

### 1. Lack of Financial Transparency
- The administration’s repeated delays in releasing a clear financial transparency plan have eroded trust among all stakeholders.
- Recent investigative reports and leaks have revealed alarming discrepancies in the university’s accounting practices, raising questions about oversight and accountability.
- The absence of detailed public financial statements has left the community in the dark regarding the true scope of the crisis.

### 2. Proposed Tuition Increases
- The proposed tuition hike risks placing an undue burden on students and their families, many of whom already struggle with the rising cost of education.
- Alumni fear that higher tuition will deter prospective students, further diminishing the university’s reputation and enrollment numbers.

### 3. Threats to Academic Integrity
- The potential layoffs of faculty members and department mergers threaten the quality of education and research, which are the cornerstones of our university’s mission.
- Faculty morale has been severely impacted, as evidenced by the formal petition submitted by the science department on December 11, 2025.

### 4. Neglect of IT Infrastructure
- Recent network outages and reports of outdated hardware have highlighted the university’s failure to invest in critical infrastructure.
- Continued neglect in this area will disrupt academic activities and further tarnish the university’s reputation.

### 5. Failure to Engage Stakeholders
- The administration has not sufficiently engaged with students, faculty, or alumni in seeking solutions to the crisis.
- The December 10, 2025, emergency town hall meeting exposed the administration’s lack of preparedness and inability to address community concerns effectively.

---

## Impact on the Alumni Community

The alumni community has been profoundly affected by the university’s financial crisis in the following ways:

- **Reputation Damage:** The ongoing media scrutiny and negative press have tarnished the university’s standing, diminishing the value of our degrees.
- **Alumni Giving:** Many alumni who have historically contributed to the university are now hesitant to donate, citing concerns over financial mismanagement and lack of accountability.
- **Community Trust:** The disconnect between the administration and stakeholders has strained the relationship between alumni and the university.

---

## Recommendations

The alumni association urges the administration to take the following actions:

### 1. Immediate Financial Audit
- Commission an independent, third-party audit of the university’s finances to identify and address discrepancies.
- Publicly release the findings to restore transparency and rebuild trust.

### 2. Stakeholder Engagement
- Establish regular forums for dialogue with students, faculty, and alumni to ensure their voices are heard in decision-making processes.
- Create a dedicated task force with representatives from all stakeholder groups to develop a comprehensive recovery plan.

### 3. Prioritize Academic Excellence
- Commit to preserving faculty positions and academic programs to maintain the university’s reputation for excellence in education and research.
- Explore alternative cost-cutting measures that do not compromise the core mission of the university.

### 4. IT Infrastructure Investment
- Develop a phased plan to modernize the university’s IT systems and infrastructure, ensuring long-term reliability and security.

### 5. Transparent Communication
- Provide regular updates to the university community on the progress of financial recovery efforts.
- Set clear deadlines for the release of financial reports and adhere to them.

---

## Conclusion

The alumni association stands in solidarity with students, faculty, and staff who are advocating for a brighter future for our university. We recognize the immense challenges facing the administration but believe that these challenges can only be overcome through transparency, collaboration, and a steadfast commitment to the university’s core values.

We call on the administration to act decisively and inclusively to navigate this crisis and restore confidence in the institution. The alumni community remains ready to support these efforts, provided they are guided by accountability, integrity, and a genuine commitment to the well-being of all stakeholders.

Together, we can work to ensure that the university emerges from this crisis stronger, more resilient, and better prepared to serve future generations.
