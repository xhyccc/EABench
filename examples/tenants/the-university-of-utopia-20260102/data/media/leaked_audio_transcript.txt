**[Unidentified Voice 4]:** And the layoffs?

**[Interim Chancellor]:** (pauses) Yes, those will be announced at the same time. We’re looking at around 50 faculty positions, give or take. It’s unfortunate, but necessary.

**[Unidentified Voice 3]:** This is going to blow up in our faces. You know that, right?
