# Board Member Resignation Notes

**Author:** Alejandro Rodriguez  
**Date:** December 9, 2025  
**Context:** Notes taken during a private meeting where a second board member hinted at stepping down, citing concerns about the university's current trajectory. These notes are confidential and intended for internal use only.

---

## Meeting Details

- **Date:** December 9, 2025  
- **Time:** 3:00 PM - 4:30 PM  
- **Location:** Conference Room A, Administrative Building  
- **Attendees:**
  - Interim Chancellor Maria Lopez
  - Board Chairperson Evelyn Carter
  - Board Member David Kim
  - Board Member Laura Patel
  - Board Member Michael Greer
  - Board Member Elizabeth Chang
  - University Counsel James Carter
  - Secretary Alejandro Rodriguez (Note-taker)

---

## Key Agenda Items

1. **Update on Financial Transparency Plan**
   - Interim Chancellor Lopez opened the meeting by addressing the delayed release of the financial transparency plan.
   - Explanation provided: Additional accounting discrepancies discovered required further review.
   - Board members expressed concern about public backlash and the growing mistrust among students, faculty, and alumni.

2. **Discussion on Leadership Stability**
   - Chairperson Evelyn Carter highlighted the unexpected resignation of Board Member Rachel Simmons on December 8, 2025, citing personal reasons.
   - Concerns raised about the impact of leadership changes during a period of financial and operational instability.

3. **Potential Second Resignation**
   - Board Member Michael Greer expressed frustration with the university's trajectory under current leadership.
   - Key points raised by Michael Greer:
     - Lack of a clear long-term strategy to address the budget deficit.
     - Concerns over the proposed department mergers and potential layoffs.
     - Disillusionment with the administration's handling of student and faculty unrest.
     - Perceived lack of alignment among board members regarding institutional priorities.
   - Greer indicated that he is seriously considering stepping down from his position unless significant changes are made in the coming months.
   - Greer’s remarks:
     > "I’ve poured years into this institution, but I’m not convinced we’re heading in the right direction. If we can’t come together and establish a sustainable path forward, I don’t see how I can continue to serve effectively."

4. **Contingency Planning**
   - Chairperson Carter emphasized the need to prepare for potential further board resignations.
   - Suggested actions:
     - Expedite the search for new board members to fill recent and potential vacancies.
     - Develop a clear communication plan to address potential public concerns over board instability.

5. **Next Steps**
   - Interim Chancellor Lopez committed to:
     - Finalizing and releasing the financial transparency plan within the next week.
     - Holding additional consultations with faculty and student representatives to address ongoing concerns.
   - Board members agreed to reconvene in one week (December 16, 2025) to review progress on key issues.

---

## Observations and Notes

- Atmosphere in the meeting was tense, with clear divisions among board members regarding the university’s approach to resolving the financial crisis.
- Board Member Elizabeth Chang appeared sympathetic to Michael Greer’s concerns but did not explicitly suggest she might also resign.
- University Counsel James Carter advised caution in public communications, warning against statements that could exacerbate tensions with students and faculty.
- Interim Chancellor Lopez appeared visibly strained, indicating the mounting pressure on her leadership.

---

## Action Items

### For the Interim Chancellor:
- Expedite the financial transparency plan and ensure it addresses concerns raised by various stakeholders.
- Meet with key faculty and student leaders to rebuild trust and gather input on alternative budget solutions.

### For the Board:
- Begin identifying potential candidates to fill existing and anticipated vacancies.
- Develop a unified public message to reassure the university community about the board’s commitment to stability and long-term sustainability.
