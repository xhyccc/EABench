# Proposal: Hosting a Live Q&A Session for the New Product Launch

**Author:** Priya Patel  
**Date:** December 29, 2025  

---

## Overview

As part of the new product launch strategy, this proposal outlines the plan to host a live Q&A session to engage with our customer base, address their concerns, and build excitement around the product. Given the recent challenges faced by the company—including server issues, app slowdowns, and mixed reactions to the leaked promotional video—this session represents a proactive step toward rebuilding trust and generating positive momentum ahead of the product launch.

## Objectives

The live Q&A session aims to achieve the following:

1. **Direct Engagement:** Provide a platform for customers and stakeholders to interact directly with the product team, addressing questions and concerns in real time.
2. **Transparency:** Reinforce the company's commitment to openness and customer-centric values.
3. **Excitement:** Build anticipation and excitement for the new product, leveraging the opportunity to highlight key features and benefits.
4. **Feedback:** Gather valuable user feedback to identify potential areas for improvement before the official launch.

## Proposed Format

- **Platform:** Conduct the session on [platform options: Zoom, YouTube Live, or Instagram Live].
- **Duration:** 60 minutes.
- **Moderation:** A senior marketing associate will moderate the session to ensure smooth transitions and filter inappropriate or off-topic questions.
- **Participants:**
  - **Panelists:**
    - Product Manager
    - Lead Developer
    - Customer Support Representative
  - **Audience:** Existing customers, early adopters from the beta program, and the general public.

## Key Topics

The live Q&A will focus on the following topics:

1. **Product Features:** Highlight key functionalities and unique selling points of the new product.
2. **Pricing Model:** Address customer concerns about affordability and value.
3. **Technical Stability:** Reassure users about server capacity improvements and app reliability.
4. **Future Roadmap:** Provide a sneak peek into upcoming updates and features.

## Implementation Plan

### 1. **Preparation Phase**
- **Timeline:** January 1–January 10, 2026
- **Tasks:**
  - Finalize the platform and logistics for the session.
  - Develop a FAQ sheet to cover anticipated questions.
  - Create promotional materials (social media posts, email newsletters, and website banners) to announce the event.
  - Conduct technical rehearsals to ensure a smooth experience for both panelists and attendees.

### 2. **Promotion Phase**
- **Timeline:** January 11–January 20, 2026
- **Tasks:**
  - Launch a social media campaign using hashtags related to the product and event (e.g., #AskUsAnything, #ProductLaunch2026).
  - Send email invitations to beta users and newsletter subscribers.
  - Partner with influencers and key opinion leaders to amplify reach.

### 3. **Execution Phase**
- **Date:** January 21, 2026
- **Tasks:**
  - Conduct the live Q&A session.
  - Monitor audience engagement and respond to questions in real time.
  - Collect feedback from attendees through post-event surveys.

### 4. **Follow-Up Phase**
- **Timeline:** January 22–January 25, 2026
- **Tasks:**
  - Publish a summary of the session on the company blog.
  - Share key highlights and Q&A highlights on social media.
  - Analyze attendee feedback and incorporate insights into the product launch strategy.

## Risk Assessment

- **Technical Issues:** Risk of platform downtime or connectivity problems.  
  - **Mitigation:** Conduct thorough testing and have IT support available during the session.

- **Negative Feedback:** Risk of receiving critical or hostile questions.  
  - **Mitigation:** Prepare responses to anticipated negative feedback and moderate the session effectively.

- **Low Attendance:** Risk of limited audience participation.  
  - **Mitigation:** Invest in targeted promotion and leverage existing user groups and communities.

## Expected Outcomes

1. Increased customer trust and satisfaction.
2. Enhanced product awareness and interest among the target audience.
3. Actionable feedback to refine the product before launch.

## Conclusion

Hosting a live Q&A session offers a unique opportunity to connect with our audience, showcase the strengths of our new product, and address any lingering concerns directly. By investing in transparency and engagement, we can position the company as a trusted leader in the industry and pave the way for a successful product launch.
