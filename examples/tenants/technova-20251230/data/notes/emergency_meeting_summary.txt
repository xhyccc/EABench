# Emergency Meeting Summary

**Author:** Sarah Nguyen  
**Date:** December 29, 2025  
**Context:** Summary of the emergency meeting led by the CEO addressing the botnet attack.

---

## Overview
The emergency meeting was convened by the CEO to address the ongoing botnet attack that has severely impacted server performance, user experience, and the company's operational stability. The meeting focused on identifying immediate action plans, long-term strategies, and fostering team collaboration during this critical period.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. **Current Situation and Impact**
- **Botnet Attack:**
  - The IT team confirmed that the unusual traffic spikes over the past two days were caused by a botnet attack.
  - The attack has resulted in intermittent server outages, degraded app performance, and increased user complaints.
  - A key client threatened to cancel their contract due to repeated app issues, highlighting the growing business impact.

- **System Vulnerabilities:**
  - The temporary optimization solution implemented by the IT team improved performance slightly but revealed deeper issues with database queries and server architecture.
  - Intermittent problems with app notifications and slowdowns continue to strain resources.

### 2. **Immediate Actions**
- **External Cybersecurity Consultants:**
  - The CEO announced that external cybersecurity experts will be brought in to assist the IT team in mitigating the attack and fortifying defenses.

- **Temporary Measures:**
  - The IT team will implement additional traffic filtering mechanisms to minimize the botnet's impact.
  - A task force comprising senior IT and engineering personnel was formed to monitor and respond to emerging threats in real time.

- **Communication Plan:**
  - Customer support will send out a company-wide notification to users, apologizing for the disruptions and reassuring them that steps are being taken to resolve the issue.
  - The marketing team will coordinate with customer support to craft messaging that minimizes reputational damage.

### 3. **Mid-Term Strategy**
- **Infrastructure Upgrades:**
  - The engineering team outlined plans to address underlying database and server bottlenecks identified during the attack.
  - A proposal to migrate critical services to a more resilient cloud-based infrastructure was discussed.

- **Employee Training:**
  - The CEO emphasized the importance of cybersecurity training for all employees to prevent future vulnerabilities, including accidental code pushes and data leaks.

### 4. **Long-Term Initiatives**
- **Security Framework:**
  - The leadership team agreed on the need to establish a comprehensive cybersecurity framework with periodic audits and stress tests.

- **Cross-Team Collaboration:**
  - The CEO stressed the importance of fostering collaboration across IT, product, and customer support teams to ensure rapid response and unified efforts during crises.

- **Client Relationship Management:**
  - A dedicated account management team will be established to proactively address client concerns and prevent contract cancellations.

---

## Action Items

### **IT Team:**
- Implement advanced traffic filtering mechanisms to mitigate botnet impact.
- Coordinate with external cybersecurity consultants to conduct a full security audit.
- Initiate a plan to address database query inefficiencies and server bottlenecks.

### **Customer Support Team:**
- Send out a clear, empathetic communication to users regarding the disruptions.
- Prioritize resolving high-impact user complaints and escalate critical cases.

### **Marketing Team:**
- Develop messaging to reassure users and preserve the company's reputation.
- Monitor social media channels closely for sentiment shifts and address concerns in real time.

### **Leadership Team:**
- Finalize the selection of cybersecurity consultants and approve the necessary budget.
- Establish a cross-functional task force to oversee crisis management.
- Evaluate long-term proposals for infrastructure upgrades.

---

## Meeting Outcomes
- Immediate measures were agreed upon to mitigate the botnet attack and stabilize operations.
- Plans for infrastructure upgrades and long-term security enhancements were initiated.
- A renewed emphasis on collaboration and resilience was communicated by the CEO.

---

## Closing Remarks
The CEO closed the meeting by acknowledging the team's hard work amidst the crisis and encouraging open communication across all departments. The company’s resilience, combined with external expertise, will serve as the foundation for overcoming this challenge.
