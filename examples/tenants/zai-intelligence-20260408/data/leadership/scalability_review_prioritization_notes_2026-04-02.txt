## Discussion Highlights

### 1. Scalability Review vs Debugging Tasks

**CFO's Position:**  
- Emphasized the need to prioritize scalability review as a critical deliverable for investors.
- Argued that delays in addressing scalability could jeopardize long-term funding and trust.

**CTO's Position:**  
- Stressed that debugging tasks (especially related to memory leak and latency issues) are blocking critical milestones.
- Warned that ignoring these issues could lead to cascading failures during scalability testing.

**Key Points Raised:**
- The temporary workaround for the memory leak is insufficient for high-load scenarios; scalability review would likely uncover further bottlenecks.
- Engineering bandwidth is already stretched thin, raising concerns about the feasibility of balancing both tasks.
- A junior engineer's suggestion to leverage the undocumented feature for latency improvements sparked debate over whether to allocate resources for experimentation.

### 2. Resource Allocation Conflicts

**Product Manager's Concerns:**
- Highlighted the urgency of finalizing the benchmark dataset demo to meet investor expectations.
- Requested additional engineering support to resolve the visualization bug and dataset inconsistencies.

**Data Science Lead's Response:**
- Pushed back on reallocating data science resources, citing ongoing efforts to address inconsistencies in the training dataset.
- Proposed pausing non-critical tasks to free up resources, but this was met with resistance from other teams.

**HR's Input:**
- Shared findings from the latest employee survey:
  - Widespread dissatisfaction with current communication and leadership alignment.
  - Concerns over burnout and conflicting priorities.

### 3. Proposed Solutions

#### Short-Term:
- **Scalability Review Prioritization:**
  - Allocate a small, focused team to begin scalability testing while the rest of the engineering team continues debugging efforts.
- **Benchmark Dataset Demo:**
  - Cross-functional collaboration between engineering and product teams to address critical visualization bugs within the next 48 hours.
  - Data science team to conduct a rapid quality check on the benchmark dataset to ensure investor readiness.
- **Communication Improvements:**
  - HR to host a follow-up town hall meeting (with clearer communication and scheduling) to address employee concerns and clarify the 'no-meeting' policy.

#### Long-Term:
- **Scalability and Debugging Alignment:**
  - Establish a dedicated task force for scalability testing and debugging to prevent resource conflicts.
  - Explore the feasibility of integrating the undocumented feature from the open-source model to address latency issues.
- **Process Improvements:**
  - Implement stricter data backup protocols to prevent future data loss incidents.
  - Revise internal communication guidelines to reduce confusion and improve alignment among teams.
