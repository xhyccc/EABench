# Emergency Integration Plan Meeting Notes

**Author:** Hyejin Wang  
**Date:** 2026-03-13  
**Context:** Notes from the emergency meeting held to address the divide over the integration plan for new post-training techniques.

---

## Agenda

1. Review of the current state of the integration plan.
2. Addressing technical concerns raised by team members.
3. Discussing alternative approaches to resolve the divide.
4. Setting actionable next steps and deadlines.

---

## Attendees

- **CTO:** Facilitator of the meeting, responsible for the integration plan.
- **Lead RL Engineer:** Raised concerns about the feasibility of new techniques and proposed alternative hyperparameter tuning strategies.
- **Senior Data Scientist:** Flagged issues with the automated annotation tool and contributed to technical discussions.
- **Junior Engineer:** Provided insights into the undocumented feature of the open-source model.
- **CFO:** Attended to represent funding concerns and investor pressures.
- **Additional team members:** Engineers, data scientists, and product managers.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Integration Plan Status

- **CTO's Summary:**
  - Revised draft of the integration plan circulated on 2026-03-10.
  - Preliminary feedback revealed mixed opinions, with technical feasibility being the primary point of contention.
  - Investor pressure to finalize the plan and demonstrate progress is mounting.

- **Challenges Identified:**
  - Diverging opinions on adopting new post-training techniques.
  - Performance drops in recent experiments with hyperparameter tuning.
  - Lack of clarity on resource allocation for exploratory tasks related to the open-source model.

### 2. Technical Concerns

- **Hyperparameter Tuning:**
  - Lead RL Engineer reported performance drops in the latest experiment.
  - Skepticism from team members about the long-term viability of the proposed workaround.

- **Dataset Annotation:**
  - Senior Data Scientist flagged anomalies in the automated annotation tool's output.
  - Significant errors in early testing results, delaying progress.

- **Open-Source Model:**
  - Junior Engineer identified a potential workaround for the undocumented feature but inadvertently triggered a server overload during testing.
  - Senior Engineer raised concerns about the lack of documentation for critical features.

### 3. Alternative Approaches

- **Proposal from Lead RL Engineer:**
  - Revert to the previous hyperparameter tuning strategy temporarily while exploring new techniques offline.

- **Proposal from Senior Data Scientist:**
  - Allocate dedicated resources to debug and refine the automated annotation tool.

- **Open-Source Model Debate:**
  - CTO suggested postponing exploratory testing until the integration plan is finalized.
  - Mixed reactions from team members, with some advocating for immediate resource allocation.

### 4. Investor Pressure

- **CFO Update:**
  - A+-round funding delays due to requests for additional metrics and timelines.
  - Urgent need to finalize the integration plan to maintain investor confidence.

---

## Decisions Made

1. **Integration Plan:**
   - CTO will revise the plan to address technical concerns and circulate a final version by 2026-03-15.
   - Team members must provide feedback by the end of 2026-03-14.

2. **Hyperparameter Tuning:**
   - Lead RL Engineer will lead a small team to test the previous strategy alongside exploratory testing of new techniques.

3. **Dataset Annotation:**
   - Senior Data Scientist will head a focused debugging session for the automated annotation tool.
   - Decision on whether to adopt the tool will be postponed until 2026-03-17.

4. **Open-Source Model:**
   - Junior Engineer will document findings on the undocumented feature and share results by 2026-03-16.
   - CTO will decide whether to allocate further resources based on the report.

5. **Investor Relations:**
   - CFO will compile updated projections and product timeline milestones for A+-round investors by 2026-03-14.

- **Senior Data Scientist:** Debug and refine the automated annotation tool.
- **Junior Engineer:** Complete testing and documentation of the open-source model.
