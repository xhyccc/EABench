## Summary of Key Discussions

### 1. **Current Status of the Demo**
- **Visualizations:**
  - A critical bug was discovered in the visualization code during the final rehearsal.
  - The data science team is working on rewriting affected sections but requires additional time.
  - The visualization bug impacts the demo’s ability to clearly communicate key insights to investors.

- **Dataset Issues:**
  - A mislabeled subset of annotations was identified on 2026-04-06, further delaying preparations.
  - Efforts to correct the mislabeled data are ongoing but resource-intensive.

- **Technical Challenges:**
  - Partial fix for the memory leak issue introduced a minor latency spike in the agent training pipeline.
  - GPU utilization spikes caused by the fix have temporarily halted training jobs.

**Overall Assessment:**
- PM: The demo is not investor-ready in its current state.
- Data Science Lead: Requires a minimum of 1-2 additional days to address critical errors.
- Engineering Lead: Stabilization efforts are progressing, but unresolved GPU utilization issues could impact downstream workflows.

### 2. **Critical Blockers and Issues**
- **Technical Blockers:**
  - Memory leak workaround introduced new performance regressions (GPU utilization spikes).
  - Ongoing debugging of the visualization bug and mislabeled annotations.
  - Compatibility issues with the open-source model integration remain unresolved.

- **Operational Challenges:**
  - Conflicting priorities between technical and investor-facing deliverables.
  - Employee burnout and resource constraints are slowing progress.

- **Leadership Conflict:**
  - CFO emphasized the importance of meeting the investor review deadline to secure additional funding.
  - CTO argued that prioritizing technical feasibility is critical to maintaining long-term credibility.

### 3. **Debate on Delaying the Investor Review**
- **CFO's Perspective:**
  - Delaying the review risks damaging investor confidence and jeopardizing A+-round funding.
  - Suggested presenting a partial demo with a clear roadmap for resolving outstanding issues.

- **CTO's Perspective:**
  - Delivering an incomplete or flawed demo could harm the enterprise’s technical reputation.
  - Proposed postponing the review by one week to ensure a polished and functional demo.

- **PM's Input:**
  - Highlighted the risk of investor dissatisfaction but supported the CTO’s recommendation for a delay to avoid reputational damage.

- **Team Feedback:**
  - Engineering Lead: Agreed with CTO, citing the need to stabilize the infrastructure.
  - Data Science Lead: Supported the delay, emphasizing the impact of unresolved visualization bugs.
  - HR Manager: Expressed concerns about employee morale and burnout if the team is forced to rush.
