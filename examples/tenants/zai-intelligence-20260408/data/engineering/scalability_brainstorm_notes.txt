## Current Scalability Challenges

### Key Issues
- **Memory Leak in Open-Source Model Integration**
  - Emergency workaround implemented, but:
    - Introduced **latency issues** under high-load scenarios.
    - Lacks long-term viability.
  - Engineering team flagged the need for deeper debugging and refactoring.
- **Latency Problems**
  - Identified during stress testing of the memory leak workaround.
  - Current inference times exceed SLA targets by 30% during peak usage.
- **Dataset Management**
  - Recent data loss incident due to accidental deletion by an intern.
  - Recovery efforts surfaced **minor inconsistencies** in training datasets.
  - Quality control measures are insufficient for current demands.
- **Resource Constraints**
  - Ongoing debates over prioritization of tasks:
    - Benchmark dataset demo preparation.
    - Performance optimizations.
    - Exploration of experimental tools and techniques.
- **Team Dynamics**
  - Low attendance at critical brainstorming sessions due to overlapping deadlines.
  - Anonymous employee survey revealed dissatisfaction with leadership communication and policy changes.

## Proposed Solutions

### 1. Refactoring Open-Source Model Integration
- **Action Items:**
  - Conduct a thorough code review to identify root causes of memory leaks.
  - Explore potential optimizations using the undocumented feature discovered on 2026-04-01.
  - Assign a dedicated sub-team to focus solely on resolving integration issues.
- **Risks:**
  - High time investment.
  - Potential for introducing new bugs during refactoring.

### 2. Latency Optimization
- **Potential Approaches:**
  - Leverage the undocumented feature in the open-source model for **inference speed improvements**.
  - Implement **asynchronous processing** for certain high-latency tasks.
  - Evaluate the feasibility of **sharding** to distribute loads more effectively.
- **Risks:**
  - Implementation complexity.
  - Requires additional time for testing and validation.

### 3. Dataset Quality and Recovery
- **Proposed Strategies:**
  - Automate **data backup processes** to prevent future incidents.
  - Introduce **checksum-based validation** for datasets to ensure integrity post-recovery.
  - Experiment with the **alternative annotation strategy** proposed by the junior data scientist to speed up recovery.
- **Risks:**
  - Resource conflicts with other ongoing initiatives.
  - Alternative annotation strategy is untested at scale.

### 4. Policy and Process Improvements
- **Suggestions:**
  - Improve communication around policy changes (e.g., 'no-meeting' policy) to avoid confusion and dissatisfaction.
  - Schedule brainstorming sessions with clear advance notice to improve attendance.
  - Address employee burnout through more flexible deadlines and workload balancing.
- **Risks:**
  - Requires buy-in from leadership.
  - May slow down short-term progress.

### 5. Experimental Library Integration
- **Proposal:**
  - Evaluate the feasibility of integrating the experimental open-source library discussed during the session.
  - Set up a small-scale prototype to test potential benefits before committing significant resources.
- **Risks:**
  - Diverts attention from critical path issues.
  - May add undocumented dependencies.
