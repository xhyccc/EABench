# Cybersecurity Incident Log

## Author: Mosi Kabongo

### Context:
This log details an attempted login from a foreign IP address tied to a known data breach network. The incident occurred amidst heightened cybersecurity concerns at Bertrand and Co., as the firm navigated mounting challenges related to Gens Chemistry Ltd.'s IPO preparation.

---

## Incident Details

### **Date:** 2026-04-05  
### **Time:** 14:23 UTC  
### **Severity Level:** High

### **Description:**
Bertrand and Co.'s IT department flagged an unauthorized login attempt originating from a foreign IP address (93.184.216.34) identified as part of a known data breach network. The attempt targeted the firm's internal document server, which houses sensitive information related to Gens Chemistry Ltd.'s IPO, regulatory compliance records, and client communications.

---

## Technical Details

### **IP Address:**
- **Origin:** 93.184.216.34  
- **Geolocation:** Frankfurt, Germany (suspected proxy server)  
- **Classification:** Linked to previous data breach incidents reported in cybersecurity threat databases.

### **Target:**
- **System:** Internal Document Server (server ID: DOC-SRV-45B)  
- **Access Attempt:** Unauthorized login via admin credentials  
- **Protocol:** HTTPS  
- **Encryption:** Attempted bypass of TLS 1.3 encryption layer  

### **Detection:**
- **Monitoring Tool:** Intrusion Detection System (IDS) - version 5.3.2  
- **Alert Code:** ALRT-4587-HIGH  
- **Timestamp of Detection:** 2026-04-05, 14:23 UTC  

---

## Action Taken

### Initial Response:
- **Firewall Update:** Blocked the foreign IP address and its associated subnet to prevent further attempts.
- **Access Lockdown:** Temporarily restricted login access to the internal document server for all non-essential users.
- **Incident Notification:** Alert issued to all senior IT personnel and cybersecurity consultants.

### Investigation:
- **Log Analysis:** Reviewed server access logs for anomalies and potential signs of prior intrusions.
- **Threat Assessment:** Conducted cross-referencing with cybersecurity threat intelligence databases to confirm the IP's association with known breach networks.
- **Vulnerability Scan:** Initiated a full scan of the document server for potential exploits or unpatched vulnerabilities.

### Mitigation:
- **Credential Audit:** Forced password resets for all admin accounts on the document server.
- **Software Update:** Applied urgent patches to the server's operating system and encryption protocols.
- **Enhanced Monitoring:** Deployed real-time monitoring scripts to flag unusual activity across all document servers.

### Communication:
- **Internal Memo:** Sent to all staff outlining the incident and reinforcing proper cybersecurity practices.
- **Consultant Engagement:** External cybersecurity consultant assigned to oversee the investigation and recommend long-term security improvements.

---

## Findings

### **Key Observations:**
- The attempted login utilized a brute-force attack method, targeting admin credentials.
- No evidence of successful access was found during preliminary investigations.
- The foreign IP address belongs to a network flagged in multiple cybersecurity reports for phishing campaigns and ransomware distribution.

### **Risks Identified:**
- **Data Exposure:** Potential risk to confidential IPO documents and internal communications.
- **Reputation Damage:** Possible fallout if the incident escalates and becomes public knowledge.

---

## Recommendations

### **Short-Term Actions:**
- Conduct mandatory cybersecurity training for all staff with a focus on phishing prevention and password hygiene.
- Perform a comprehensive audit of all document-sharing protocols and access controls.

### **Long-Term Actions:**
- Upgrade the firm's intrusion detection and prevention systems (IDS/IPS) to the latest available version.
- Implement multi-factor authentication (MFA) across all systems to enhance login security.
- Schedule regular penetration testing to identify and remediate vulnerabilities proactively.
