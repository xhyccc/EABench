\section{Extended Experimental Results}
\label{app:extra_results}

This appendix collects supporting tables that accompany the main results in
Section~\ref{sec:experiments}. Table~\ref{tab:wilcoxon_full} reports the full
pairwise Wilcoxon signed-rank test matrix on paired cases (used in
Section~\ref{sec:experiments} to substantiate that architectural gaps are
statistically significant on some tenants but not others).
Table~\ref{tab:threshold_sensitivity} reports pass-rate stability as the
assertion-match threshold~$\tau$ is varied from $0.70$ to $1.00$, supporting
the robustness claim in the Evaluation Metrics subsection.
